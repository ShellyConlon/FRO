ABOUT FRO

FRO blends timeless tailoring with modern style to create premium menswear for every occasion.

Founded on a passion for quality fabrics and confident design, our collections celebrate sharp silhouettes, bold details, and effortless elegance.

From tailored suits to refined casual wear, FRO delivers pieces made to look great and feel exceptional.

Our global ateliers collaborate with multi-generational master weavers to harvest zero-compromise wools, robust linens, and pristine silks. Every construction detail undergoes rigorous fitting phases to guarantee unmatched durability and comfort.
